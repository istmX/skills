# The @istmx/skills Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and hyper-collaborative environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are building the future of **Agentic AI Orchestration**, and we believe that the best AI tools are built by diverse, respectful, and highly collaborative human communities.

## The AI Collaboration Standard

Because this repository deals directly with Artificial Intelligence and LLM Orchestration, we have specific standards for contribution:

1. **Knowledge Sharing**: We welcome all contributions that make our AI skills smarter. If you have built a great prompt template, a new motion workflow, or a structural framework rule, share it! We encourage opening PRs to refine our `SKILL.md` rules.
2. **Deterministic Quality**: When proposing changes to the AI instructions, aim for deterministic, anti-slop rules. We want to guide the AI to be highly professional, not generic.
3. **Ethical AI Usage**: We do not support modifying these skills to generate malicious code, spam, or harmful content. 

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
* Sharing knowledge openly (e.g., sharing how you crafted a perfect AI prompt).
* Being respectful of differing opinions and coding paradigms.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery.
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
* Weaponizing the AI orchestrators for malicious intent.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
